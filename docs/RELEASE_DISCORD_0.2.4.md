**PEMF 0.2.4 — Officers and Crew**

**Named officers.** A new line in every port: *Manage yer crew!* Six posts, three standings. You're paying for the **search**, not the man — whether anyone turns up is a roll, and the gold goes either way.

Every officer is generated. A name, a history, one to three talents, and maybe a **flaw**. *Light Fingers* skims your prizes. *A Loose Tongue* gets your disguise seen through. *More Butcher Than Surgeon* means men who'd have lived don't. Cheap searches turn up flawed men far more often.

**Crew morale.** Seven states, MUTINOUS to DEVOTED — including worse than the game can express. It moves for real reasons: the officers you keep, cargo lost in a storm, time. It drives the game's own morale, and saves with your career.

**Modders:** write mods in code now — plugins, one header, in the zip. Plus your own town-menu rows and nested menus from JSON.

**Fixed:** the alt-tab crash, storm drums going silent, pausing killing them, WASD.
