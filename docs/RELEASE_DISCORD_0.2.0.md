@everyone **False Flag is here.**

PEMF's first release with a **complete new mechanic** — a system that never existed in Pirates!

Fly another nation's colours. Find out what it costs when they stop believing you.

**Install** — drop the archive into your game folder (the one with `Pirates!.exe`) and launch. **GOG and Steam both work.** The exe is never modified. Uninstall = delete `version.dll`, `pemf_core.dll` and `PEMF\`.

**WASD steering** is on by default — sailing, ship battles, land battles, duels, sneaking. Two keys had to move: **Attack → Space**, **Quick Save → F5** (load **F9**). Your old bindings are backed up.

**Playing False Flag**
`Ctrl+Shift+8` raise a different flag · `Ctrl+Shift+9` your own colours back up · `Ctrl+Shift+0` write a report to the log

Raise a flag that isn't yours and a meter appears top right. It **fills while their ships and ports can see you**, and **empties in open water**.

That's the whole thing: **a disguise is worth wearing because it lets you get close — and getting close is exactly what gives you away.**

They'll signal for your colours. Then study your rigging. Then come about.

Fill it and you're rumbled: reputation drops, **their ports close**, a **price goes on your head**, your colours are struck, and **pirate-hunters sail**. How many and how strong depends on how much they already hate you — a first offence is one ship, a long history a squadron.

Sail away and the trail goes cold. The further you get, the faster it cools.

**Two known rough edges**
• You start under **English colours** whatever faction you picked — an unfinished feature, not a bug.
• Suspicion is **tuned fast on purpose** so you can see the whole arc in a minute. Everything is in `PEMF\suspicion.ini` — commented, and reinstalling won't overwrite your edits. Lower the three `rise` values if you want it subtler.

**Bugs:** post `pemf.log` from your game folder with a sentence about what you were doing. That's where everything useful is.
